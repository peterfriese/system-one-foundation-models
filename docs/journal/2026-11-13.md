## 🚧 Challenges & Solutions

### 1. Reconciling Parallel Branch Evolution (`origin/main` PRs #10, #17, #18)
- **Challenge**: Parallel merges on `origin/main` introduced `GeminiFoundationModels` (claiming Tech Note `0018`) and `ProxyCredential` / `SystemOneProxyBackend` in `SystemOneCore` while our audit branch had introduced `ProxyTransport` in `SystemOneCore` and numbered the homogeneity note `0018`.
- **Solution**: Rebased onto `origin/main` (`815b37e`), standardized on PR #18's richer zero-dependency `ProxyCredential` + `SystemOneProxyBackend` across all 6 providers (retaining `ProxyTransport` in `JevFoundationModels` for backwards compatibility), homogenized `GeminiFoundationModels` onto `SystemOneExecutor`, and cleanly renumbered the homogeneity tech note to `0019`.

### 2. Eliminating Demo Sprawl Without Losing Domain Coverage
- **Challenge**: Maintaining 7 separate vendor-specific CLI targets (`ticket-triage-demo`, `clef-demo`, `openai-demo`, `gemini-demo`, etc.) duplicated argument parsing and locked each domain workflow to a single vendor.
- **Solution**: Separated concerns into 6 single-file `< 10`-line `Quickstarts/` (for instant copy-paste onboarding) and 1 unified `system-one-cli` (`Examples/CLI/`) pairing 4 domain subcommands with a 7-mode `ProviderFactory`, letting developers benchmark any workflow against any backend with `--provider <mode>`.

### 3. Multi-Turn Vision Transcript Bloat & Cross-Platform iOS/visionOS PKZIP Extraction
- **Challenge**: Full-transcript image extraction re-sent Turn 1 images on later turns (hitting the 4-image guardrail), while `/usr/bin/ditto` `Process` extraction in `MailTriageApp` was unavailable on iOS/visionOS.
- **Solution**: Sliced `TranscriptAttachmentExtractor` strictly after the most recent `.response` entry and implemented a native central-directory PKZIP + `zlib` raw-deflate extractor with CRC-32 and path-traversal validation in `CoreMLModelManager`.
